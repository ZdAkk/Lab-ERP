The spine of the data model

One unbroken chain runs from the protocol to the price. The protocol says what happens on which day, a phase resolves to a recipe, the recipe explodes into components, each component is a material, materials arrive as lots with expiry dates, lots are consumed by media preparations, and that consumption becomes a cost line. If any link is modelled wrongly, the reference calculation cannot match.

The documents

Every field carries a status. BRIEF is stated by Systasy or the problem brief. PROPOSED is our structure, to confirm or reject. OPEN is unknown and left unfilled, pointing at a question. No domain values are invented anywhere.
