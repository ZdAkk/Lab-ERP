# R-01 Protocol → phase → step

- Protocol 1 : n Phase (ordered by sequence). PROPOSED.
- Phase 1 : n Step template. PROPOSED.
- Step may depend on another step (precedence with min/max gap in days). PROPOSED.

Rules
- Phase day ranges must not have gaps; overlaps OPEN (Q-P01).
- Steps that cross phases (e.g. replating at a phase boundary) belong to the phase in which they start (PROPOSED).
- Readout steps (R-09) attach to the protocol at an anchor step with offset.
