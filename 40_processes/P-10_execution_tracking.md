# P-10 Execution and tracking

Out of scope for the 2-day prototype (PROPOSED), but part of the product story.

Steps
1. Daily task list per person/lab (O3 filtered by date).
2. Media preparation: confirm prep, pick lots (scan), movement OUT, preparation gets expiry.
3. Feeding / other tasks: mark done / skipped / deviated.
4. Discard expired preparations and lots (movement OUT discard).
5. Deviation (e.g. failed plate, delay) → replan from current state (Q-PL03).
6. Actual vs planned cost.

Matches the Systasy job ad: media and reagent preparation, stock keeping, ELN documentation.
